What I cover

Infrastructure & safety

Fire-safety compliance in high-rise buildings, construction standards, bridges and embankments — who signed off, who inspected, and what the paperwork actually shows.

Rivers & environment

The decline of Dhaka's waterways, encroachment and industrial discharge, and the long environmental cost of projects built across the country's rivers.

Public interest & welfare

Whether government programmes reach the people they were written for — traced through records, data and the households left off the list.

About

Reporting from Dhaka since 2019

I'm a multimedia journalist with more than five years in digital and broadcast newsrooms, working across investigative research, data reporting, video production and interactive storytelling.

My work starts in the field and ends in the documents: interviews and on-the-ground reporting, checked against records, datasets and satellite imagery. I've built interactive projects and data visualisations to carry findings that don't fit in prose, and I produce my own video packages from script through edit.

I currently report for Netra News and work as an independent consultant with OCCRP. I write and report in both Bengali and English.
